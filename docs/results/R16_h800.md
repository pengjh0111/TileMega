## Reproduction and closure gaps

Use `scripts/reproduce_r16_cell.py` for one frozen native candidate or baseline
cell. It defaults to strict PID ownership, emits a queue, and runs only with
`--run`. Supply actual GPU/NUMA; `--cpu-bind-only` records unavailable membind.
`scripts/run_r16_dnn_candidates.py` prepares full-model DNN candidate/gate queues.
`scripts/summarize_r16.py` rejects incomplete/failed evidence and separates DNN
candidate/binary/checkpoint identities. `scripts/collect_r16_candidates.py`
requires exact actual dispatch, three fresh final processes and source contracts;
pilot-only results cannot become selected defaults. `scripts/compare_r16_results.py` pairs
only complete protocols; DNN exports must match, LLM unpaired random weights
are labelled, and speedup>1 means faster TileMega. Raw evidence and generated artifacts
remain ignored under `runs/r16/`; small committed result CSVs stay below1 MB.
