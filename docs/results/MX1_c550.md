Deviations, all **verified** unless noted:

1. **Last-arriver handoff off** (`--nonpaged-la 0`, both phases), as the
   MX-C/MX-T cells that validated this port ran. A prefill plan carrying it is
   refused at load (`ServingRuntime.cuh` needs `TILEMEGA_SERVING_SEQ==1`), and
   on decode it hit §9.1.
2. **Row-major non-paged weights** (both phases). `ServingGemvTaskBody`'s
   xcore1000 specialisation rejects the tiled layout
   (`static_assert(!Tiled)`), decode at M≤4 dispatches to it, and a cell's two
   plans share one process, so both must agree.
3. **torch.compile MoE experts run eager.** This MetaX torch has no
   `torch._grouped_mm`; the harness probes for it, falls back, and records
   `grouped_mm_error` in the measurement (commit `217024176`).
4. **Baselines: one round for the MoE cell, two for the dense cells.** The
   three-round protocol was reduced to one on request; rounds 1–2 were already
   complete for the dense models, so both are reported.
5. `counted_dependency_cg` fails at `test/unit/counted_dependency_cg_test.cpp:105`.
   **Inferred pre-existing**: the test and `BuildDependencyTableLinear` last
   changed before the baseline, and no commit on this branch touches the
   host-side lowering it asserts on. Verifying would need a build at
   `243fe9d06`.

## 7. Performance

Protocol: ISL/OSL 64/1024, B ∈ {1,2,4,8,16}, random weights, median over
rounds, one job per card under `gpu_guard`. TTFT from the N=1 run, E2E from
N=OSL, TPOT=(E2E−TTFT)/(OSL−1), throughput=B·OSL/E2E,
MBU=(weights+KV)/(TPOT·1.64 TB/s). TileMega and the baselines compute these
with the same formula (`serving/measure.py:139`,
`serving/baseline_common.py:103`, `vllm_baseline.py:101`), so the ratios are
like-for-like (**verified**).

TileMega cells measured so far (**verified**, median of 2 rounds). Full
per-cell TTFT/TPOT/E2E/throughput/MBU for every round, TileMega and baselines,
is in `runs/mx1/results/serving.csv` (168 measurements).

## 8. GPU usage (§0.3)

**Verified** from `runs/mx1/gpu_jobs.log`, appended by the guard wrapper on
every job: 602 guarded jobs, **9.83 GPU-hours** — GPU 7 (154 jobs, 7.77 h),
GPU 6 (441, 1.94 h), GPU 5 (7, 0.12 h). GPUs 0–3 never touched, at most two
cards at once, one job per card, all through `gpu_guard`. GPU 6 carries a
solve's short device queries (300 s cap each); GPU 7 the timed cells.

Guard changes after it refused 21 valid cells (`091909e4d`, **verified**):
wait for a device to settle rather than refusing on the first busy sample;
widen the idle-power margin to the drift seen at 0% utilization (97 W nominal
vs 130 W sampled); size the hidden-memory budget per job, since the driver
attributes only part of a 31 GiB job's footprint and the MoE baseline flagged
itself.

## 9. Unfinished, with causes

1. ~~Decode plans with last-arriver do not build.~~ **Fixed** (commit
   `a8a846c9e`). `Codegen.cpp` emitted the literals `TILEMEGA_GEMM_TILE_K 16`
   and `STAGES 3` — `GemmGranularity`'s defaults — for any module carrying
   `tilemega.g`, which the last-arriver path sets. Legal where `k%16==0`
   suffices, illegal on xcore1000, whose serving GEMM body allows k ∈ {64,128}
   (`BackendCostQuery.h:135`), so `cucc` failed on
   `static_assert(ServingBF16ShapeLegal)` for
   `ServingGemmConfig<Xcore1000,16,32,16,3>`. The solver never chose that
   shape: its candidate domain only enumerates k ∈ {64,128}
   (`OperatorClasses.h:101`), and codegen discarded the selection. Now the
   representative's real `tile_k`/`stages` travel through the attribute.
   **Verified**: the same cell that failed (`qwen3_0_6b` B1 decode, la=1,
   `NONPAGED_LAST_ARRIVER selected=112`) now emits `TILE_K 64, STAGES 4` and
   builds. The grid itself still ran with `--nonpaged-la 0` (deviation §3.1),
   since it was already in flight when the fix landed.
2. **Llama-3.2-3B does not solve, in either phase** (all 10 cells,
   **verified**). 24 q over 8 kv heads is a GQA ratio of 3, splitting ISL 64
   into 21-row chunks with a ragged tail, so a per-task count is 10 for three
   tiles and 5 for the last:
   `{ [m,g] -> ((10 - 5*floor((5m)/16)) + 5*floor((4 + 5m)/16)) : … 0<=g<=7 }`.
   `FlowPreparation.cpp:139` needs one scalar per stage, so
   `QuasiPolynomial::Eval` throws; decode reports `max 80 != min 40`.
   Independent of `--attention-impl` and of last-arriver. Next step: teach
   `evaluate_count` to use `EvalPoints` per position, or price the padded tail
   the hardware actually executes. Filtering the query-row tile list for
   divisibility does *not* help: `group*serving_seq` is 192, which 16, 32 and
   64 all divide.
3. **MoE and DNN** are outside MX-1, run as extra scope: the ResNet18 DM plan
   compiled (`tasks=24 couplings=31`, **verified**); the Qwen3-MoE-24L decode
   solve was still searching. Its torch.compile baseline reached only B1/B2/B4
   before the 2.5 h cap — dynamo hit its recompilation limit (§3.3).
4. **Paged executor** not exercised; §5 permits deferring it.
