**Verified tail calculation.** In the 28,672-column gate, TN96 and TN192 fail exact N divisibility. TN8–TN64 require at least five waves at grid 108. TN128, 224 and 256 have respectively 8 tasks in a third wave, 20 in a second wave and 4 in a second wave. For the 16,384-column Llama-3.2-3B gate, TN128 gives 20 tasks in a second wave and TN256 fits all 64 tasks into one wave. The CPU domain sweep checked all candidate widths and the replay charged the busiest worker rather than an average occupancy fraction.

**Inferred.** Widening TN does reduce the number of CTAs with an extra task. At the large-model geometry, the larger tile's local service outweighs that wave effect. The Llama-3.2-3B dimensions show the opposite behavior.
