## 1. Reproducibility (MX3-0, G-REPRO)

The harness now reads the committed target, its guard and C-1/C-2 wrappers are
in `scripts/`, and every path derives from the script (**verified**).
`MX1_serving_c550.csv`'s `note` had described MX-2's implementation on MX-1's
measurements; it is rebuilt from each campaign's own build command, and
`MX3_serving_c550.csv` labels every row with `campaign`, `layout` and `impl`.

The first single-HEAD rebuild of MX-2 drifted by up to +5.3%, and the cause
was the pin, not the device (**verified**): `--evaluate-configs` could carry
neither the attention block (`SkeletonEvaluationCase` had no `Ec`/`Rq`) nor a
fixed residency (after compiling, `SkeletonSearch.cpp` re-ranked residency up
to the compiled limit, so a "residency 2" pin came back at 4). Both are fixed
(`016505672`). **TM-MX2**, the rebuild with all three pinned:

| cell | MX-2 | TM-MX2 |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 5.748 | 5.750 |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 7.560 | 7.568 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 4.699 | 4.705 |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 15.994 | 15.965 |
| Qwen3-1.7B B16 | not measured | 9.244 |

Within 0.2% on every cell MX-2 measured. A "clean clone" was not exercised;
the TM-MX2 tree is a worktree at `e9e4e5ae0` plus the pin fix and the inert
trace hook, built with its own compiler.

Two process faults cost time, both mine and both caught before a bad number
entered a table. A compiler binary embeds its source root
(`TILEMEGA_SOURCE_DIR`): device headers, resource probes and the artifact
identity are read from there, so copying a binary into a frozen worktree froze
nothing, and an edit in the main tree failed in-flight builds with
`source changed during compilation`. Every final-run plan is built by a
compiler built inside its own tree. And the MACA objects under `build/maca`
are custom commands without header dependencies, so a stale
`ServingTaskCalibration.o` hid two calibrators that no longer built.

## 2. Where the time goes (MX3-1)

**Primitives** (`MX3_primitives_c550.csv`, 22 probes, **verified**):
dependent DRAM read **363 ns** (calibration 345), dependent contended
`atomicAdd` **230 ns** (calibration 215; independent adds pipeline at 9.9 ns),
V3 grid barrier **471 ns** at 104 CTAs and **678 ns** at 208, `__syncthreads`
14.7 ns (calibration 27.0), shuffle 53 ns, `__nanosleep(0)` 534 ns, one trace
clock read 126-201 ns. **F11 is refuted**: latency is ~363 ns, not ~1.06 µs.

**Stage ledger** (`MX3_stage_ledger_c550.csv`, `scripts/mx3_stage_ledger.py`,
the runtime's own stage and task traces at past 575). Instrumentation costs
TPOT +5-12% stage-only and +19-26% with task profiles (**verified**), so
shares below are from stage-only traces. Each stage's envelope splits exactly
into bandwidth bound + inefficiency + imbalance + barrier:

| cell | step | bound | GEMM | combine | attention | merge | barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 6.37 | 0.78 | 57% | 22% | 18% | 3.6% | 0.21 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 4.90 | 1.55 | 74% | 11% | 12% | 2.7% | 0.11 |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 17.18 | 9.37 | 82% | 6.6% | 10% | 1.4% | 0.22 |

(ms, sum of stage envelopes.) The excess over the bound is 69-75%
inefficiency, 21-44% imbalance and **only 3-4% barrier**: per layer
200-244 µs, matching F10. Inside a GEMM stage on the critical CTA (task trace,
Qwen B1) a task waits 6-12 µs from stage entry to start and 7-10 µs for its
first operand tile, against 6-11 µs of body and 2-4 µs of epilogue.

**Resources** (`MX3_resources_c550.csv`): every megakernel entry point sits at
248 MTregisters; no isolated body does (max 224, a 64x128 depth-4 GEMM; the
SIMT decode attention is 138-202). The ceiling is the megakernel, not a body.

## 3. GEMM mainloop pipeline (MX3-2, G-PIPE)

F1 and F2 were exact: `ServingGemmMma` had no `Stages`, the loop ran one block
at a time, and pricing divided latency by `stages-1` and also dropped
`stages-1` iterations, so 27 of 149 MX-2 tiles claimed depth 8-10 on a body
with only a 4-way unroll. `Stages` is now the number of 32-K chunks in flight,
held in registers (`index_sequence` fold so ring slots are compile-time; a
runtime index put the ring in local memory at 5.8x cost). MMA order is
unchanged, so C-2 stays bit-exact; `test_mx_task_bodies.py` covers every legal
depth (**verified**). No MACA disassembler is installed (`llvm-objdump` is
absent; the device ELF decodes as ARCv2.3), so "the compiler does not wait for
all" is shown behaviourally: depth 1→6 gives 755→226 ns/load, monotone.

Model and body now agree in eight places: depth bounded by registers
(`ServingGemmDepthFits`, 32 pipeline VGPRs) instead of the shared memory the
body never staged; depth 1 legal and priceable (two guards threw below 2);
the latency discount and the in-flight bytes count chunks, not `stages-1`
(depth 1 had been priced at **108.95 ms** for a 5.96 ms plan); the prewarm
list, the calibrators, the DM body (kept at its pre-MX-3 overlap) and the
tests follow. The task-body segment was re-run at depth 1 (GPU 7, 10.3 s,
2026-10-20); DRAM, L2 and sync constants are untouched.

**G-PIPE is met.** At MX-2's geometry, Ec and residency, only depth varying:

| cell | depth 1 | depth 2 | depth 4 | depth 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 (Ec 256) | 5.958 | | 5.987 | 7.205 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 5.268 | 4.776 | **4.592** | |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 20.72 | | | |

Depth helps where tasks hold long reductions (Llama-3.2-1B gate_up: 64 chunks
per task) and not where they hold 4-16 (Qwen3-0.6B), and depth 6 spills
(stack 232 → 376 B, +21%), which set the 32-VGPR budget. The probe agrees:
its 3.34x at one CTA per AP falls to 1.5x at four. Restricting the domain to
depth 1 on the Qwen evidence alone, which I did first, cost Llama-3.1-8B 30%;
the domain offers every depth the budget admits and measurement decides.

## 4. Decode attention through MMA (MX3-3, G-ATTN)

`PrefillAttentionMma` at Tokens=1 already carried the decode contract (RoPE,
q/k-norm, KV write by the owning block, per-block partial and LSE, direct
context at `cmax==1`), so mma16 is a selection, not a new body. It is priced
from its own fits (`fused_attention_decode_mma16_*`) and refuses to compile
without them. It needs 11.5-38.5 KiB of shared memory against SIMT's ~2 KiB,
which caps residency at 2 for D=128.

C-1/C-2 pass on Qwen3-0.6B B1 and B16 and Llama-3.2-1B B1 (0 mismatches over
512 steps in four modes; C-1 max gap 0.016-0.094). Llama-3.1-8B ran out of
device memory inside the C-2 harness and is not verified. A/B, depth 1, MX-2's
Ec:

| cell | simt | mma16 | residency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 5.890 | 6.191 (+5.1%) | 2 |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 8.354 | 13.376 (+60%) | 2 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 (D64 Q4) | 5.268 | **5.130 (−2.6%)** | 4 |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 24.75 | 24.85 (+0.4%) | 2 |

mma16 wins only where D=64 lets it keep residency 4. **G-ATTN is not met**:
`TILEMEGA_ATTENTION_IMPL` is a whole-plan macro chosen on the command line, so
the search cannot weigh it per cell, and the final run keeps simt.

## 5. Execution and synchronisation (MX3-4)

Ordered by the ledger. **Residency (item 1)**: F8 does not hold; the selected
plans already run residency 3-4 (grid 312-416), so there is no 2-wave regime
to lift. Residency 4 → 2 at fixed geometry costs Qwen B1 3.9% and 8B 19%.
**Barrier protocol (item 2)**: 3-4% of the excess, the smallest term; not
changed, so no litmus was needed. **LA (item 3)**: builds again, and
decomposed at matched residency 3 on Qwen B1 it costs **+4.3-4.9%** from the
protocol itself against +0.4% from losing residency 4; one of three LA runs
also hit a device `Trap Assert`. Off. **Epilogue (item 4)**: 4-5% of the
critical CTA's span; not attempted. **Partition (item 5)**: less split-K is
slower on both cells tried (Llama-3.2-1B 5.347 → 5.758 at ≤2 → 6.557 unsplit;
Qwen B1 +4.8% unsplit); combine stages are the price of parallelism.

## 6. Selection (MX3-5)

Decode is chosen by measurement: at most 4 candidates, one pilot round, the
top 2 timed twice (`--measure-finalists`, `--measure-rounds`). Prefill keeps
the predicted choice. A shortlisted plan whose placement is position-dependent
(16x256 at residency 3, max 32 != min 16 tasks per worker) used to abort the
whole search after 700+ evaluations; it is now recorded as
`MATERIALIZE_FAILED` and dropped. In the depth-1 trial the pilot spread
reached 1.7x and the model's first choice lost on Qwen B1 (5.57 vs 5.36 ms).

## 8. Reference ceilings (MX3-7)

**Vendor BLAS** (`F.linear`, the call vLLM-MetaX makes; `MX3_vendor_gemm_c550.csv`):
small decode GEMMs floor at ~13 µs; large ones reach 99-103% of 1.61 TB/s.
TileMega's stage for the same class takes 2.0-2.35x that on the two small
models and 1.21-1.94x on 8B. **mcTlass** (`MX3_mctlass_c550.csv`): MX-2 timed
the template defaults, a SIMT GEMM on Sm70 with a 128x128 block. The tensor-op
path does not assemble on MACA (inline PTX `ldmatrix`,
`arch/memory_sm75.h:250`); MetaX-native mainloops cover int8 and w4a16 dense,
and bf16 only as grouped/MoE 128x128; the SIMT path tuned for M ≤ 16 reaches
270-450 µs, 2.5% of DRAM; `-O3` builds still crash on the host. Its native
kernels stage B in registers and wait with `arrive_gvmcnt(n*(stages-1))`,
the mechanism MX3-2 uses.
