## 4. What changed the result on H800

- verified: the Codex RF2 candidates used a fixed seed geometry (TN128, TK64, two stages, split-K 1).
  Narrow GEMMs ran on 16–24 of 132 SMs; Llama-8B B1 TPOT was 14.4 ms against vLLM 6.85 ms.
- `--seed-geometry` (`include/tilemega/Solver/ServingSeedGeometry.h`) picks per GEMM family a tile, pipeline depth and the
  smallest power-of-two split-K that reaches ≥118 tasks; SwiGLU tiles are narrowed instead of split; split residual GEMMs
  below TN128 are rejected for mma16 (verified C-1 failure, max logit gap 1.30, Qwen3-0.6B — recorded, gate not relaxed).
- Decode WGMMA keeps `stages` operand slots in flight (`ComputePipelinedWgmma`). The two-slot loop issued one weight tile per
  wgmma wait; expert tasks streamed ~5 GB/s per SM (verified trace).
- Candidate policies and flags: `configs/serving/seed_geometry/*.json`, `configs/serving/r16_h800_candidates.json`.
  Selection: 1024-token pilot per cell over ≤8 candidates, then C-1/C-2 + 8 zeroed repeats, then three fresh timing processes.
- Rejected (verified): split SwiGLU GEMMs race under the device loop (8/8 repeat mismatches); MoE Ec64 fails C-2 and crashed;
  forward split-K seed for ResNet18 is slower (1.21 vs 1.18 ms); WGMMA on Qwen dense is slower than mma16 TN32.
- Not a default change: these are explicit, recorded per-cell selections; the solver has no cost-model entries for them (§9).

## 7. Where the time goes (stage traces at past 575, diagnostic only)

- Llama-8B B1 (7.27 ms step, mma16 TN128 split): gate_up 2.73 ms (132 SMs), other projections 1.82, qkv 0.77,
  attention 0.61 (72 SMs), split-K combine 0.45 + gaps, lm_head 0.36. Weight floor 4.48 ms (stated bandwidth) → MBU 0.73.
- Qwen0.6B B1 (2.11 ms, 227 stages): attention 0.45, gate_up 0.41, other projections 0.31, qkv 0.22, combine 0.18, merge 0.10,
  lm_head 0.12. Weight floor 0.36 ms; inferred: the remainder is per-stage latency (~0.7 µs release + ~0.4 µs gap × 227 stages
  plus sub-µs tasks), which a stage-serial megakernel cannot hide at this size.
- MoE B1 (11.5 ms, 676 stages): expert/dense GEMMs 3.0 ms, attention 2.3 ms on 20 SMs, split-K combine 2.1 ms, top-k 1.1 ms on
  one SM, RMSNorm 0.6 ms on one SM, MoE combine 0.5 ms. inferred: top-k, norm and attention parallelism are the next targets.
- Large-batch dense (Qwen B8/B16): attention (QPerKV 2, D128) dominates; the best attention candidate is SIMT with Ec256.

## 9. Unfinished and next steps

1. **MoE B≥4 intermittent failure (pre-existing).** Codex's H10 configuration and this round's grouped and slot binding all
   show, at B4/B8/B16, rare token mismatches (first diff at step 100–650) or CUDA launch failures (≈1/50 generations; e.g.
   Codex config B4: 1 mismatch + 1 crash in 5 fresh × 9 runs). B1/B2 are clean (45/45 repeats, C-1/C-2 PASS). Suspects:
   expert-indirect GEMM + DM last-arriver reduction (`include/tilemega/Codegen/executor/DmReduction.cuh`,
   `MoEDispatchTaskBody.h`) under L1 stage barriers. MoE B4/B8/B16 timings are reported but not accepted (†).
2. **Split SwiGLU race** under the L1 device loop (`ServingGemmCombineTaskBody.h` path): avoided by policy, not fixed.
3. **No cost-model entries** for the seed policies or the multi-slot WGMMA; RF2 selection here is explicit pilot + E2E.
   H §5.0-7 (solver fits) is open; B-2's full serving fit also failed cross-card.
4. **DNN**: the forward plans are latency-bound (39 ms NAFNet, 1.2 ms ResNet18); the skeleton forward search does not finish in
   50 min. Next: bounded forward search, stage fusion, per-layer parallelism.
5. **Small models**: Qwen0.6B MBU 0.2 — per-stage latency dominates (§7); needs fewer stages (fusion of combine/norm) rather than faster bodies.
6. MoE: single-SM top-k and RMSNorm, 20-task attention; cluster H-3/H-4d stay off. Ownership is provisional (PID namespace);
   `MultiGPU_plan.md` was not supplied, so no multi-GPU plan is written.
