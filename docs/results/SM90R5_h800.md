## 3. Phase B: tensor parallel implementation

Single process, T devices, one plan per rank from one generated library, CUDA
P2P (plan §3.2). o and down reduce their tile inside the epilogue as 8-byte LL
packets carrying (two bf16 values, epoch); the head exchanges one
(value, global index) per row and its release/acquire also orders the next
step's writes after this step's reads, so the all-reduce path carries no fence
(plan §3.5 C2).

TP1 is unchanged by construction: the degree, the AR slot and the head's
vocabulary are written only when `tp > 1`, so a TP1 coordinate still serializes
as schema 6 and its generated source is byte-identical.

### 3.1 Correctness (verified)

| check | TP2 | TP4 | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR + head exchange unit test, bitwise | pass | pass | `sm90_dense_tp_test`, 8 AR cases (both epilogue paths, TN 8/16/32, B1/B16) + 3 head cases |
| mutation control | fails as designed | — | reversing the tie rule and dropping per-rank bf16 rounding gives 4096 errors |
| litmus, 1024 tokens, 5 launches | 5/5 identical | 5/5 identical | perturbed step 1.515 vs 1.227 ms (TP2), 1.353 vs 1.075 ms (TP4) |
| C-1 vs HF, 1024 positions | pass | pass | B1 and B16 per degree; every position inside the near-tie threshold |
| C-2, 1024 tokens, 5 launches | bitwise identical | bitwise identical | B1 and B16 per degree |
| executor loop vs per-launch | exact | exact | same tokens from both execution paths |
| per-rank token agreement | enforced | enforced | asserted by `TpServingEngine` on every generation |
| dense gate | pass | pass | source byte-identical and ranking unchanged at every commit |

C-1 maximum gaps: 0.03125 at TP2 B1/B16 and TP4 B16, 0.015625 at TP4 B1 — one
bf16 step or less against a single-card HF reference at every one of the 1024
positions (`SM90R5_tp_final.csv` carries the per-cell verdicts).

The executor comparison found a real defect in the harness, not in the device
code: `TpServingEngine` enqueued one rank's whole step range before the next
rank's first kernel, but a rank's step-s kernel spins in its first all-reduce
until every peer's step-s kernel runs, so the driver's finite per-stream launch
queue filled and the host blocked with the peers never enqueued — a deadlock
that only the non-loop path reaches. Fixed by enqueueing one step across all
ranks before advancing (verified: 128 tokens in 9.2 s, tokens identical to the
loop path). No measured cell is affected: every TPOT here uses the step loop,
which enqueues a single kernel per rank.

## 4. Open finding: the TP cost model under-predicts, and mis-ranks the tiles

All four measured candidates at TP2 B1 carry qkv TN16 and gate_up TN32 and
differ only in lm_head. A coordinate carried over from REF-R3's TP1 winner
(qkv TN32/3 slots/WK1, gate_up TN48) is 21.9% faster, and it never entered the
top 4. Both are legal in the space (qkv TN32 gives 64 tasks at TP2, inside the
admitted 33…264), so this is a ranking error in `Sm90DenseCostModel`, not a
pruning gap. Per §1.5 the coordinate was not fixed by hand and the model was
not re-fitted; the τ control below is reported instead.

The control used the same protocol, group and checkpoint, 3 launches each, so
only the coordinate differs (verified):

| cell | solver winner | REF-R3-derived | gap | spread | prompt target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP2 B1 | 1.4993 | **1.2302** | 21.9% | 0.159% | 1.00–1.15 ms |
| TP4 B1 | 1.3079 | **1.0771** | 21.4% | 0.036% | 0.75–0.95 ms |

With the unranked coordinate TP4 B1 reaches 1.467× the fastest TP4 engine and
TP2 B1 1.584×, i.e. inside the round's 1.4–2.0× expectation, against 1.208× and
1.300× for the selected winners (all against TRT-LLM, fastest at every TP
cell once its recovered rounds are in). The TP mechanism therefore meets the ratio
goals at both degrees; the shortfall against every target in §3.3 is the cost
model's ranking, measured at 21–22% at both degrees.

The same inversion appears inside the solved cells: TP2 B4 (1.3887) is 7%
faster than TP2 B1 (1.4993) and TP4 B8 (1.2752) is faster than TP4 B1 (1.3079),
although TPOT should rise with batch. At B4 and B8 the search does reach the
wider gate_up and o tiles it misses at B1 and B2.

The model also under-predicts the step everywhere, and more so with more ranks
(`SM90R5_tp_solver.csv`, µs per step):

| B | TP2 pred | TP2 meas | τ | TP4 pred | TP4 meas | τ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1019.9 | 1499.3 | 1.470 | 791.9 | 1307.9 | 1.652 |
| 2 | 1036.0 | 1509.1 | 1.457 | 824.0 | 1321.2 | 1.603 |
| 4 | 1066.4 | 1388.7 | 1.302 | 842.0 | 1349.4 | 1.603 |
| 8 | 1113.6 | 1580.7 | 1.419 | 906.8 | 1275.2 | 1.406 |
| 16 | 1242.4 | 1584.4 | 1.275 | 1062.0 | 1528.3 | 1.439 |

Mean τ 1.463. The cross-rank terms are not the cause: at TP4 B1 the measured
`t_ar` gives 56 × 1.728 µs = 97 µs and the exchange 3.5 µs against a 516 µs
shortfall. The model's 791.9 µs also reproduces the plan's own §3.10 estimate
(~740 µs), so the unaccounted time sits in the per-stage fixed cost at these
shapes, not in the TP mechanism. Localizing it needs D0 marks on a TP program,
which this round scoped only onto the MoE path.

## 6. Environment findings (verified)

- **Idle power recalibrated.** GPUs 0–4 hold 1980 MHz SM clocks while idle (set
  outside this harness), drawing 129.5–142.9 W against the 81.5–87.8 W recorded,
  so no group could start there; idle values updated, no clock changed.
- **GPU 6 excluded from timing**, per user instruction, then re-scoped for TP8
  (§7).
- **MPK Qwen3-30B-A3B B16** is an MPK defect, not memory:
  `multitoken_paged_attention_hopper_impl` computes wrong attention whenever
  MAX_TOKENS × queries-per-KV exceeds 64 (16 × 8 = 128 here), reaching the
  argmax as NaN, whose `-1` equals the `eos_token_id=-1` sentinel, so every
  request stops at step 64. Localized on two-layer random models teacher-forced
  against HF: 16 slots match 1–5 of 128 tokens, 8 slots 124 of 128 (gap ≤ 0.047,
  bf16 ties); a dense Qwen3 with the same head counts fails identically, so the
  MoE kernels are not involved, and Llama-3.1-8B (4 queries per KV) is correct
  at 16 slots. B16 is therefore collected at the demo default of 8 slots,
  25.5895 ms, where a 16-request step takes two kernel iterations — not a
  like-for-like B16, and labelled so in `SM90R5_mpk.csv`.
- **OLMoE torch.compile** was never a torch.compile measurement in 4.57.1:
  OLMoE has no experts interface there, its per-expert `torch.where` breaks
  fullgraph on `aten.nonzero`, every expert iteration's resume frame recompiles
  until dynamo's limit (8), and the rest runs eagerly — 92–143 ms, host-CPU
  bound, moving 46% with host load. Under the R16 MoE setup (transformers
  5.20.1, `grouped_mm`, fullgraph) it is 3.0361 / 3.9906 / 5.2785 / 7.5639 /
  12.3558 ms, 1.83–2.42× TileMega (`SM90R5_olmoe_torchcompile.csv`).

## 7. TP8: functionally verified, timing withheld (verified)

Requested by the user after TP2/TP4, with the question whether GPU 6 throttles
constantly or only once hot. Measured answer (`runs/sm90r5/tp/gpu6_thermal.json`,
10 segments of a fixed bf16 matmul load with 15 s idle gaps):

- **Idle**: 80 °C, 1830 MHz, 194 W, every throttle reason inactive, against
  31–46 °C and 82–143 W on the other cards. So it does not throttle constantly.
- **Under load**: `sw_thermal_slowdown` went Active in **all 10 segments**, each
  only 0.33–0.48 s long, at 89–90 °C, with SM clocks falling to 990–1635 MHz
  against the 1980 MHz its peers hold. One segment also raised
  `hw_thermal_slowdown` and `hw_slowdown`. Throughput spanned 342–494 TFLOPS,
  a 30.8% range.
- **Recovery**: 15 s idle restores 81–82 °C and 1830 MHz, but the next segment re-throttles at once.
Segmenting cannot avoid it: the onset is faster than any useful unit of work
(a 1024-token decode runs ~1.5 s), and §1.6 voids a throttled round anyway. No
TP8 TPOT is published and the T = 8 constants were not measured.

Correctness does not depend on clocks, so the 8-rank protocol was verified
instead, at fixed coordinates (per-rank qkv N 512 → TN8, gate_up N 1536,
down K 768, vocabulary 18992 → TN16; queries per KV head is 16/8 = 2 at every
degree, so the attention shape and its 64-chunk merge carry over):

| check | result |
|---|---|
| AR + head exchange unit test, 8 ranks | 0 errors over 8 AR cases and 3 head cases |
| C-1 vs HF, 64 positions | pass, max gap **0.0** at every position |
| token stability, 2 runs | identical |
| per-rank agreement | all 8 ranks identical every generation |
| litmus, 1024 tokens, 5 launches | **5/5 bitwise identical** (step 1.0755 → 1.3536 ms perturbed) |

The eight ranks' kernels start within about 1.5 ms of each other and run
concurrently, so the single-process, T-device launch model holds at degree 8.
