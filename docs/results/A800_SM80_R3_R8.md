# A800 SM80 R3 R8 · producer and consumer role ABI

**Stated decision rule.** Consider a separate role ABI after the measured body, layout and pricing work closes. A change to CTA participation needs a separate arch variant with its own guard, residency audit, barrier proofs and calibration. Compare against the 160-thread production path under the frozen 40-cell protocol.

**Verified evidence.** Current SM80 uses 32 lanes for `cp.async` copies and 128 consumer threads for MMA and epilogues. The measured activation issue rate is 12.88 GB/s per CTA against 32.46 GB/s per CTA for bulk DRAM access. Qwen30 B1 `347719` recorded 40,649 producer cycles for gate/up issue and publication and 28,177 consumer cycles waiting for `full`. The trace perturbed the measured window by 20.7%, so those role counters are diagnostic. The current campaign's 216-CTA dense and OLMoE service probes demonstrate effective overlap within the 160-thread ABI. [Role trace](A800_SM80_R2.md) · [Residency measurements](A800_SM80_R3_R3.md)

**Inferred decision.** Preserve the current ABI. It supports the measured grid and body work and has a complete set of guarded MMA prices. Changing the producer warp count would change ring arrival counts, shared-memory layout, launch bounds and the calibrated kernel's participation contract. No alternative ABI was measured in this round, so the current result supplies no performance ceiling for such a redesign.
