# MX-3 on MetaX C550: dense decode TPOT

Spec SHA256 `1535ebe6d84fc74fe6f46527d19970eb357910ee09683bef1f188e5891e49fba`;
branch `tilemega-metax` from `207e51901`. Claims are **verified**, **stated** or
**inferred**. TPOT follows MX-1 (ISL/OSL 64/1024, 2 fresh processes × warm-up
1 + 2 timed, median, under `gpu_guard`); rows in `MX3_serving_c550.csv`. Two
finals: `mx3` (10-20); with the budget relaxed, `mx3-rerun` (`26632fa26`) and
`mx3-rerun2` (`c11ff37bc`, frozen tree, `scripts/mx3_final_run.sh`), reported in §7.

## 1. Reproducibility (MX3-0, G-REPRO)

The harness reads the committed target; wrappers and drivers are in `scripts/`;
paths derive from the script; every serving row carries `campaign`, `layout`,
`impl`. The first single-HEAD rebuild drifted +5.3% because `--evaluate-configs`
could not pin `Ec`/`Rq` and re-ranked residency (fixed in `4407030f8`). TM-MX2,
pinned, matches MX-2 within 0.2% (Qwen3-0.6B B1 5.750 vs 5.748, 8B B1 15.965
vs 15.994; Qwen3-1.7B B16 9.244, which MX-2 never measured).

**G-REPRO met.** TM-MX2's build inputs are on branch `mx3-tm-mx2` (`e9e4e5ae0`,
trace hook, pin fix, `configs/pins/tm_mx2/`); a clean clone of it, built and run
by `scripts/` alone, measured Qwen3-0.6B B1 at **5.758 ms**, C-1/C-2 passing
(**verified**). Faults of mine, caught before a table: a copied compiler reads its
build root (frozen trees need their own build); disconnects left extra Claude
sessions on the tree (stopped, merged, terminated).

## 2. Where the time goes (MX3-1)

**Primitives** (`MX3_primitives_c550.csv`, **verified**): dependent DRAM read
**363 ns**, contended `atomicAdd` 230 ns, V3 grid barrier 471/678 ns at 104/208
CTAs, `__syncthreads` 14.7 ns, shuffle 53 ns, `__nanosleep(0)` 534 ns. A
dependent load through a generic pointer (flat, `LDX`) costs what a global one
(`LDG`) does: 139/222/308 vs 144/230/310 ns at 4 KiB/256 KiB/16 MiB, 3 processes
× 21 interleaved launches. **F11 is refuted.**

**Ledgers** (`MX3_stage_ledger_c550.csv`; tracing costs +5-29%; the rerun's is a
heavier stage+task trace), envelope over stages, ms, TM-MX2 → first → rerun:

| cell | stages | envelope | bound | excess: inefficiency / imbalance / barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 283 → 255 → 227 | 6.37 → 4.81 → 4.59 | 0.74 | 79 / 15 / 6% (rerun) |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 147 → 147 → 115 | 4.90 → 4.09 → 3.92 | 1.54 | 73 / 22 / 5% |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 291 → 323 → 323 | 17.18 → 16.53 → 17.17 | 9.32 | 69 / 27 / 4% |

Untraced, each stage class was costed with diagnostic masks that drop or empty
it (`TILEMEGA_DIAG_*`, never measured as a plan; `MX3_stage_cost_c550.csv`).
Qwen3-0.6B B1 (first-final bodies, 4.53 ms): GEMM tasks **2.45 ms**, attention
0.70, combines **0.81** (84 stages, 9.6 µs each), merges 0.20, bare **stage
skeleton 0.76 ms** (3.0 µs × 255); a GEMM task spent 2.7 µs, a combine 2.4 µs,
on descriptors before any data. Control overhead, not bytes, was ~1.3 ms (§5, §6).
After §5-§6 (rerun plans): GEMM mainloop 1.35 ms, epilogue 0.19, decode 0.15.

**Compile facts** (`MX3_resources_c550.csv`, `MX3_mir_c550.csv`): with no MACA
disassembler, `-mllvm -print-after=stack-frame-layout` gives the final code
(`scripts/mx_mir_report.py`; `ARRIVE n` waits, `addrspace 5` local). No MMA
mainloop touches local memory; a GEMM task stores its operand block to the
stack once per task (13 stores on 8B). Entry points sit at 248 MTregisters.

## 3. GEMM mainloop pipeline (MX3-2, G-PIPE)

F1/F2 were exact: no `Stages` in the body, a double discount in pricing, 27 of
149 MX-2 tiles at depth 8-10 on a 4-way unroll. `Stages` is now the 32-K chunks
in flight, in registers with compile-time ring slots; MMA order is unchanged
(C-2 bit-exact); body tests cover every legal depth. Model and body agree in
eight places (depth ≤ 4 and ≤ 48 pipeline VGPRs, depth 1 priced, in-flight
bytes per chunk, prewarm, calibrators, DM, tests). **G-PIPE met**, MX-2's
geometry with only depth varying:

| cell | depth 1 | depth 2 | depth 4 | depth 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 (Ec 256) | 5.958 | | 5.987 | 7.205 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 5.268 | 4.776 | **4.592** | |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 20.72 | | 16.789 | |

**The wait, in machine code.** The hand wait keeps the newest chunks in flight
(`ARRIVE 70`: 6 loads at depth 4), but the guarded prefetch is a control-flow
join and the MetaX inserter adds a wait for every load (`ARRIVE 4160`) once per
group: the ring drains every `Stages` chunks. An exact-wait form (unconditional
loads, global-space operands) measured −0.6/+0.2/+0.1% on Qwen B1, Llama-1B B1,
8B B1 and **+4.7%** on Qwen B16 (variants put it on the loop restructure), so
the guarded form stays (`6023bd3d2`). Write-up B (`experiments/mx3/smem_ring.patch`:
each lane's fragments through `ldg_b128_bsm` into its own ring slots) passes the
body tests, but mxcc did not finish compiling it into the megakernel (30 min at
16 KiB, 25 min at 8 KiB; 25 s in the test library), so B was never timed in a plan.
Twice I narrowed the depth domain on too little evidence (two finals discarded).

## 4. Decode attention through MMA (MX3-3, G-ATTN)

`PrefillAttentionMma` at Tokens=1 carries the decode contract, so mma16 is a
selection priced from its own fits; its 11.5-38.5 KiB of shared memory caps
residency at 2 for D=128; C-1/C-2 pass. A/B: Qwen B1 +5.1%, Qwen B16 +60%,
**Llama-1B B1 −2.6%** (D64, residency 4), 8B B1 +0.4%.

**G-ATTN met.** `--attention-impl auto` (the harness default) searches once per
body the target can hold, keeps each body's best plan in the shortlist, and
measurement picks per cell; pins carry the body. In the rerun mma16 won
**Llama-3.2-1B B1, B2 and B4**; simt the other 17 cells (`MX3_exec_gap_c550.csv`).

## 5. Execution and synchronisation (MX3-4)

**Descriptors under the barrier.** While thread 0 waits at the grid barrier,
the second wave copies the next stage's descriptor and the GEMM invocation its
first task reads into shared memory, keyed by the global entry; each step's
`Params` is copied once; partials, residuals, norm sums and KV load through
the global space. Same pinned selections, one card each:

| cell | first final | + descriptor cache | + Params copy, KV loads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 4.551 | 4.278 | **4.036 (−11.3%)** |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 6.745 | 6.490 | **6.218 (−7.8%)** |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 3.991 | 3.726 | 3.726-3.772 (−6%) |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 16.090 | 15.714 | 15.716 (−2.3%) |

C-1/C-2 and the body tests pass. After it the skeleton is 2.8-3.3 µs per stage
and a combine task 6.5-6.8 µs. **Residency (1)**: plans run 3-4 per AP, so F8's
2-wave regime is not there; 4 → 2 costs Qwen B1 3.9%, 8B 19%. **Barrier (2)**:
0.5-0.7 µs of the skeleton; unchanged, so no litmus. The watchdog's poll costs
1.2% (same card); kept. **LA (3)**: at matched residency +4.3-4.9% from the
protocol and one device `Trap Assert` in three runs; off. **Epilogue (4)**: the
whole GEMM epilogue is 5-6% of TPOT (diagnostic); split-K partials now go
straight from the MMA registers to memory (`691cfd9b2`): −1.4/−0.5/−0.1/−1.1%
(Qwen B1, Llama-1B B1, Qwen B16, 8B B1). Rejected after A/B, each slower or
mixed on the four cells: descriptor cache for every task of a CTA (+1.2-2.5%),
larger or double-buffered SIMT attention chunks (+0.2-11.8%), warming the next
stage's weights into L2 under the barrier (+1-5%), eight backend codegen
options (none beyond noise on both cells); residency 4 is out of reach (16 KiB
dynamic + 672 B static task memory). **Partition (5)**: at fixed tiles less
split-K was slower; with combines priced honestly the solver now splits less
on its own (§6; 28 and 32 fewer stages on the 0.6B and 1B cells).

## 6. Selection and cost model (MX3-5)

Decode is measured (≤ 4 candidates, each body's best first, one pilot, top 2 ×
2); prefill is predicted. The first final's exec gap was **1.48** (MX-2 1.24): the
model charged 0.6 µs per stage boundary and ~1 µs per combine against 3.0 and
6.6 µs in situ. After recalibrating the task-body segment (GPU 7, 10.3 s),
`scripts/mx3_insitu_fit.py` fits the hop (3.03 µs) and a `gemm_combine` fit (6.65
µs) from two cells' diagnostic plans; the pilot's measured winner went 3.964 →
**3.762** (Qwen B1) and 3.660 → **3.485** ms (Llama B1). Final exec gap **1.05**
(0.95-1.25); every decode plan measure-selected, 9 of 20 not the model's rank 1.

## 8. Reference ceilings (MX3-7)

**Vendor BLAS** (`F.linear`, as vLLM-MetaX calls it): small decode GEMMs floor at
~13 µs, large ones reach 99-103% of 1.61 TB/s; TM-MX2 took 2.0-2.35x (small), 1.2-1.9x (8B).
**mcTlass** (`MX3_mctlass_c550.csv`): MX-2 timed a SIMT Sm70 default; the
tensor-op path does not assemble on MACA (PTX `ldmatrix`), native mainloops
are int8/w4a16/grouped bf16 only, decode-tuned SIMT reaches 2.5% of DRAM.

## 9. GPU use

**48.8 GPU-hours**, 1444 guarded jobs (GPU 7 28.2 h, GPU 6 20.6 h), 29.5 h of it after
the budget was relaxed; ≤ 2 cards, one job per card, all via `gpu_guard`.

## 10. Where the review was contradicted

F11 (~363 ns, not ~1.06 µs); F8 (residency already 3-4); MX-2's mcTlass verdict; the
barrier as the target (0.5-0.7 µs; per-stage control was larger). F1/F2 exact.
