ignored `runs/mx1/`; the two CSVs beside this file carry the per-cell numbers.
Claims are **verified** (measured here), **stated** (vendor or tool output) or
**inferred**.

## 2. Device conventions

**276 TFLOPS**, 64 GiB per card, 104 APs. L2 bandwidth was launch-bound and not measured validly.

## 3. Completion and deviations

S, P, B, D (the required core) complete. T complete, 5/5 TaskBody tests pass.
C and V complete for Qwen3-0.6B, the spec's required minimum (§6). E: 19 of
20 solvable cells, three of the four models complete at B1-B16 (§7). Paged
executor deferred, as §5 allows.

Deviations, all **verified** unless noted:

1. **Last-arriver handoff off** (`--nonpaged-la 0`, both phases), as the
   validating MX-C/MX-T cells ran. A prefill plan carrying it is refused at
   load (`ServingRuntime.cuh` needs `TILEMEGA_SERVING_SEQ==1`); on decode it
   hit §9.1.
2. **Row-major non-paged weights** (both phases). `ServingGemvTaskBody`'s
   xcore1000 specialisation rejects the tiled layout
   (`static_assert(!Tiled)`), decode at M≤4 dispatches to it, and a cell's two
   plans share one process, so both must agree.
3. **torch.compile MoE experts run eager.** This MetaX torch has no
   `torch._grouped_mm`; the harness probes for it, falls back, and records
   `grouped_mm_error` in the measurement (commit `217024176`).
4. **Baselines: one round for the MoE cell, two for the dense cells** — the
   three-round protocol was reduced to one on request, rounds 1–2 being done.
5. `counted_dependency_cg` fails (`counted_dependency_cg_test.cpp:105`).
   **Inferred pre-existing**: it and `BuildDependencyTableLinear` last changed
   before the baseline and nothing here touches the host-side lowering it
   asserts on; confirming needs a build at `243fe9d06`.

## 6. Correctness (C-1 / C-2)

**All 19 measured cells pass both checks** (**verified**, guard in `exclusive`
mode): Qwen3-0.6B, Qwen3-1.7B and Llama-3.2-1B at B1-B16, Llama-3.1-8B at
B1-B8. Qwen3-0.6B is the spec's required minimum (§5), so
that requirement is **met**.

**C-2** compares `L1_separate`, `L2_separate`, `L1_loop` and
`L1_loop_no_phase` over 512 steps: **0 mismatches in total**, so the four
execution modes agree token for token. **C-1** compares tokens against
HuggingFace: every cell passes (rule: all gaps ≤ 0.5), `max_gap` 0.031–0.312,
at least 84.4% of positions exactly equal. A cell needs both phases solved
since one process loads both plans; an earlier attempt died at load with
`tm_plan_create: last-arriver requires a later decode reducer` (§3.1), which
is why the grid was re-solved.

## 7. Performance

Protocol: ISL/OSL 64/1024, B ∈ {1,2,4,8,16}, random weights, median over
rounds, one job per card under `gpu_guard`. TTFT from N=1, E2E from N=OSL,
TPOT=(E2E−TTFT)/(OSL−1), throughput=B·OSL/E2E, MBU=(weights+KV)/(TPOT·1.64
TB/s) — same formula in TileMega and the baselines (`measure.py:139`,
`baseline_common.py:103`, `vllm_baseline.py:101`), so the ratios are
like-for-like (**verified**).

Every round is in [`MX1_serving_c550.csv`](MX1_serving_c550.csv), the per-cell
decomposition in [`MX1_gap_c550.csv`](MX1_gap_c550.csv). Decode runs mode L1
with `--decode-loop auto`, the only non-paged mode whose plan carries the
on-device step loop (`loop_modes=1` when the grid fits one wave); C-2
validates `L1_loop` against the other modes.

`exec gap` = measured/predicted (`predicted` is the solver's score for the
candidate it chose, `plan.so.top3.tsv`); `plan gap` = predicted/vLLM:

| Model | cells | measured ms/tok | MBU | exec gap | plan gap | vs vLLM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B | B1,2,4,8,16 | 6.01-8.40 | 10-16% | 1.20x-1.89x | 1.33x-1.50x | 1.7x-2.6x |
| Qwen3-1.7B | B1,2,4,8,16 | 8.48-11.54 | 24-25% | 1.41x-1.51x | 1.24x-1.33x | 1.8x-1.9x |
| Llama-3.2-1B | B1,2,4,8,16 | 5.49-7.79 | 22-28% | 1.35x-1.48x | 1.25x-1.58x | 1.8x-2.3x |
| Llama-3.1-8B | B1,2,4,8 | 22.34-27.27 | 37-44% | 1.52x-1.64x | 1.18x-1.25x | 1.8x-2.0x |

TileMega beats torch.compile from B2 up (5.6x at Qwen3-0.6B B16) and holds the
baselines' flat memory-bound shape, but trails vLLM and SGLang by 1.7-2.6x.
Two causes separate out, neither of them TaskBody numerics (§5, 5/5):

1. **Execution runs 1.20-1.89x slower than the solver's own model of it**, at
   every model size. Not per-step launch cost: with the on-device loop actually
   engaged TPOT moved 1.4% (5.73 vs 5.81 ms, Llama-3.2-1B B2,
   `decode_loop_used` true vs false, **verified**) — at ~6 ms a step a ~10 us
   launch cannot matter. Variant count is a partial association only (mean
   1.33x at one variant, 1.62x at five, but within-group spread is as wide), so
   it does not explain it alone (**inferred**).
2. **The solver's floor sits above what vLLM achieves**, by a margin that
   narrows as the model grows: plan gap 1.33-1.50x on Qwen3-0.6B against
   1.18-1.25x on Llama-3.1-8B, whose MBU reaches 37-44% where the smallest
   model manages 10-16%. A perfect search inside this cost model could not
   match vLLM on the small models.

Ranking: execution overhead first and uniform, cost-model floor second and
worst on small models, selection third (Qwen3-0.6B B4 got 6.01 ms with
`TILE_N 128`, B2 8.28 with `TILE_N 32`).

Baseline TPOT/TTFT/E2E/throughput/MBU per cell are in the CSV; the ratio
columns above are the comparison. Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen3-MoE-24L have
baselines but no TileMega counterpart (§9.2, §9.3).

Baseline versions and configuration (**stated**, recorded in every
measurement JSON): vLLM-MetaX (Vllm Op 0.25.0, default engine, graph capture
on); SGLang `0.5.14+detached.c432db2fb.maca3.8.2.122`
(`attention_backend=flashinfer`); torch.compile on torch
`2.10.0+metax3.8.2.2` with transformers 5.12.0 (`mode=reduce-overhead`,
`fullgraph=True`, MoE experts eager per §3.3).

## 8. GPU usage (§0.3)

**Verified** from `runs/mx1/gpu_jobs.log`, appended by the guard wrapper on
every job: 925 guarded jobs, **16.67 GPU-hours** — GPU 7 (154 jobs, 10.28 h),
GPU 5 (111, 3.79 h), GPU 6 (647, 2.56 h), GPU 4 (13, 0.04 h). GPUs 0-3 never
touched, at most two cards at once, one job per card, all through `gpu_guard`.
GPU 6 carries a solve's short device queries (300 s cap each), GPU 7 the
baseline cells and GPU 5 the TileMega measurements.

Guard changes after it refused 21 valid cells (`091909e4d`, **verified**):
wait for a device to settle rather than refusing on the first busy sample;
widen the idle-power margin to the drift seen at 0% utilization (97 W nominal
vs 130 W sampled); size the hidden-memory budget per job, since the driver
attributes only part of a 31 GiB job's footprint.

## 9. Unfinished, with causes

1. ~~Decode plans with last-arriver do not build.~~ **Fixed** (`a8a846c9e`).
   `Codegen.cpp` emitted the literals `TILEMEGA_GEMM_TILE_K 16` and `STAGES 3`
   — `GemmGranularity`'s defaults — for any module carrying `tilemega.g`, which
   the last-arriver path sets. Legal where `k%16==0` suffices, illegal on
   xcore1000, whose serving GEMM body allows only k ∈ {64,128}
   (`BackendCostQuery.h:135`), so `cucc` failed on
   `static_assert(ServingBF16ShapeLegal)`. The solver never chose that shape —
   its domain enumerates only k ∈ {64,128} (`OperatorClasses.h:101`) — codegen
   discarded the selection. The real tiles now travel through the attribute and
   the failing cell builds (**verified**).
2. **Llama-3.2-3B does not solve, in either phase** (10/10 cells,
   **verified**). 24 q over 8 kv heads is a GQA ratio of 3, splitting ISL 64
   into 21-row chunks with a ragged tail, so a per-task count is 10 for three
   tiles and 5 for the last, over kv group g and tile m:
   `{[m,g] -> ((10 - 5*floor((5m)/16)) + 5*floor((4 + 5m)/16)) : 0<=g<=7}`.
   `FlowPreparation.cpp:139` needs one scalar per stage, so
   `QuasiPolynomial::Eval` throws; decode reports `max 80 != min 40`.
   Independent of `--attention-impl` and of last-arriver. Next: teach
   `evaluate_count` to use `EvalPoints`, or price the padded tail the hardware
   executes anyway. Filtering query-row tiles by divisibility does not help:
   `group*serving_seq` is 192, which 16, 32 and 64 all divide.
3. **MoE and DNN**, extra scope. The ResNet18 DM plan compiled
   (`tasks=24 couplings=31`, **verified**). The Qwen3-MoE-24L decode solve
   first ran 25.5 h to 24,568 evaluations without selecting, because
   `--search-budget-ms` defaults to 0, leaving the search no stopping rule;
   with a 900 s budget it terminates as designed (`SEARCH_BUDGET` after 47
   evaluations, **verified**). It then fails in the resource probe on
   `static_assert(ServingBF16ShapeLegal)` for
   `ServingGemmConfig<Xcore1000,16,64,32,2>`, tile_k 32: the MoE path
   enumerates under `DmServingBF16ShapeLegal`, k ∈ {32,64,128}
   (`DmGemmTraits.h:11`), while the probe instantiates a body asserting
   `ServingBF16ShapeLegal`, k ∈ {64,128} — the §9.1 mismatch at a second site.
   Next: have the DM path instantiate the DM body, which asserts the DM rule
   (`ServingGemm.h:100`). Its torch.compile baseline reached only B1/B2/B4
   before the 2.5 h cap, dynamo having hit its recompilation limit (§3.3).
4. **Paged executor** not exercised (§5 permits it); Llama-3.1-8B B16 prefill
   hit `rc=124` at the 5 h cap after ~2600 evaluations, leaving 1 of 20 cells
   unmeasured — raise `CELL_TIMEOUT` or use one search pass.
5. **The gap to vLLM** (§7): force one variant on a 5-variant cell and
   re-measure, then audit `floor_ns` against a measured vLLM step.
