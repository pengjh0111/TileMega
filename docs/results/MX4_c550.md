**D3 in source, not by flag.** The first stack frames (456–780 B) came from IR LICM hoisting every body's
geometry out of the step/layer loops; `-mllvm -metaxgpu-disable-licm` removed them but was rejected (it masks
the cause and blocks other bodies' optimisations). Removed in source instead: thread/block ids through opaque
`StageThread()` (unsigned) / `StageBlock()` (`Backend/Mx/DecodeCommon.h`); layer and past per stage through
`UniformOpaque`, V^T merge slot through `Opaque` (`DecodeProgram.cuh`, `DecodeGemm.h`); the first group of
each GEMM segment runs unconditionally (refill index `count>0?0:-1`) since the structurizer linearizes even a
uniform `if/else` and kept both arms' rings live; attention zeroes the unused prefetch, reads `past` uniform,
dispatches blocks top-down with `>=`. Result: every plan, every bench kernel, stack 0; TPOT equals the flag
build's within noise (**verified**).

Litmus (§8, 5 fresh processes × 2000 iterations): `barrier_prefetch`, `last512`, `many` (RunMany, 8 items per
CTA) 0 violations. MIR: no 4160 wait in any block or loop of any plan.

## 3. Solver (G-SOLVER)

`MxDecodeSolver` on the measured stage table (`configs/targets/xcore1000_mx_decode.json`, re-measured on
the no-flag bodies; least-squares model for off-table shapes, R² 0.975). Measured selection builds the two
best full plans and times them end to end (2 processes each, alternating). `MX4_selection_c550.csv`:
predicted/measured TPOT **0.924–0.990** on all 20 (**G-SOLVER met**); the model is optimistic, most at B16
(0.92–0.97) where attention at L≈577 is under-read by the table's linear interpolation in L (T_c steps).
The measured pick differed from the predicted one in 2 of 20 cells (Qwen3-0.6B B2, Qwen3-1.7B B8).

## 8. Contradictions with the spec or review

1. Appendix TPOTs are 1.6–2.5× low for the small cells (Qwen B1 1.98 vs ≈1.1, B16 4.54 vs ≈1.8, Llama-1B
   2.21 vs ≈1.8); right for 8B (10.72 vs ≈10.5). The appendix assumes 1.5 TB/s from the first byte and
   3–4 µs attention; the bench bodies have 5–10 µs fixed latency per stage (§4).
2. D3's listed causes (dynamic register arrays, large by-reference structs) were not the source of the stack;
   LICM hoisting and the structurizer linearizing uniform branches were (§2).
3. §5.6 (in-place calibration at the plan's residency) was to make the prefill model rank correctly; it
   ranks residency 1 first and is wrong by up to 34% (Qwen B4), and TTFT regresses in 4 cells (§5). This
   agrees with the review's P1-3 (weight scans are not the main cause) and §2.1 (the predictor does not see
   residency's latency hiding) — the measured winners make 4× more scans at residency 2.
4. Review §2.1 expects residency 4 to be reachable once dead storage is gone; for Llama-1B prefill (D64) the
   solver then estimated 5, beyond the calibrated 1–4, so fits were missing; residency is capped at 4.
5. vLLM at Qwen3-1.7B B16 re-measures 7.4% faster than MX-1's number (other cells within ±1.5%).
