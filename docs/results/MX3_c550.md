# MX-3 on MetaX C550: dense decode TPOT

Spec SHA256 `1535ebe6d84fc74fe6f46527d19970eb357910ee09683bef1f188e5891e49fba`;
branch `tilemega-metax` from `207e51901`. Claims are **verified**, **stated** or
**inferred**. TPOT follows MX-1 (ISL/OSL 64/1024, 2 fresh processes × warm-up
1 + 2 timed, median, under `gpu_guard`); rows in `MX3_serving_c550.csv`.

## 1. Reproducibility (MX3-0, G-REPRO)

The harness reads the committed target, its wrappers are in `scripts/`, paths
derive from the script, and every row of `MX3_serving_c550.csv` carries its
own `campaign`, `layout` and `impl` (MX-1's backfilled `note` is rebuilt from
its own build commands). The first single-HEAD rebuild drifted by up to +5.3%
because `--evaluate-configs` could not pin `Ec`/`Rq` and re-ranked residency
after compiling (**verified**; fixed in `016505672`). **TM-MX2**, all pinned:

| cell | MX-2 | TM-MX2 |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 5.748 | 5.750 |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 7.560 | 7.568 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 4.699 | 4.705 |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 15.994 | 15.965 |
| Qwen3-1.7B B16 | not measured | 9.244 |

Within 0.2% on every cell MX-2 measured (worktree at `e9e4e5ae0` plus the
pin fix and the inert trace hook, its own compiler; a clean clone was not
exercised). Two process faults of mine cost time, both caught before a bad
number entered a table: a compiler binary reads headers, probes and identity
from its build root (`TILEMEGA_SOURCE_DIR`), so a copied binary froze nothing
and main-tree edits failed in-flight builds; and `build/maca` objects have no
header dependencies, so a stale object hid two calibrators that no longer
built. Every final plan is built by a compiler built in its own tree.

## 2. Where the time goes (MX3-1)

**Primitives** (`MX3_primitives_c550.csv`, 22 probes, **verified**):
dependent DRAM read **363 ns** (calibration 345), dependent contended
`atomicAdd` **230 ns** (calibration 215; independent adds pipeline at 9.9 ns),
V3 grid barrier **471 ns** at 104 CTAs and **678 ns** at 208, `__syncthreads`
14.7 ns (calibration 27.0), shuffle 53 ns, `__nanosleep(0)` 534 ns, one trace
clock read 126-201 ns. **F11 is refuted**: latency is ~363 ns, not ~1.06 µs.

**Stage ledger** (`MX3_stage_ledger_c550.csv`, `scripts/mx3_stage_ledger.py`;
the runtime's stage traces at past 575; tracing costs TPOT +5-12%, so read
these relatively). Each envelope splits exactly into bandwidth bound +
inefficiency + imbalance + barrier. TM-MX2 → final, ms summed over stages:

| cell | envelope | bound | GEMM | combine | attention | imbalance | barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 6.37 → 4.81 | 0.78 | 3.60 → 2.90 | 1.39 → 0.97 | 1.13 → 0.64 | 21 → 12% | 4 → 5% |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 4.90 → 4.09 | 1.55 | 3.62 → 2.98 | 0.55 → 0.55 | 0.58 → 0.38 | 28 → 18% | 3 → 4% |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 17.18 → 16.53 | 9.37 | 14.08 → 12.80 | 1.13 → 1.76 | 1.71 → 1.71 | 44 → 26% | 3 → 3% |

(Imbalance and barrier are shares of the excess over the bound.) TM-MX2's
excess was 200-244 µs per layer, matching F10, and barrier was the smallest
term throughout. Inside a TM-MX2 GEMM stage on the critical CTA (task trace,
+19-26%) a task waited 6-12 µs to start and 7-10 µs for its first tile,
against 6-11 µs of body. The final plans shrink GEMM and attention stages and
cut imbalance; 8B trades 1.28 ms of GEMM for 0.63 ms more combine (finer
split-K), which is why its TPOT is flat.

**Resources** (`MX3_resources_c550.csv`): every entry point sits at 248
MTregisters and no isolated body does (max 224), so the megakernel sets it.

## 3. GEMM mainloop pipeline (MX3-2, G-PIPE)

F1/F2 were exact: no `Stages` in the body, a double discount in pricing, and
27 of 149 MX-2 tiles claiming depth 8-10 on a 4-way unroll. `Stages` is now
the 32-K chunks in flight, in registers with compile-time ring slots (a
runtime index cost 5.8x in local memory); MMA order is unchanged, so C-2 stays
bit-exact, and the body tests cover every legal depth (**verified**). With no
MACA disassembler installed, the explicit wait is shown behaviourally: depth
1→6 gives 755→226 ns/load in the probe, monotone.

Model and body agree in eight places: depth bounded by registers (depth ≤ 4
and ≤ 48 pipeline VGPRs, the old mainloop's footprint) not shared memory;
depth 1 legal and priceable; the latency discount and in-flight bytes count
chunks, not `stages-1` (depth 1 had been priced at **108.95 ms** for a 5.96 ms
plan); prewarm, calibrators, the DM body and tests follow. Only the task-body
calibration segment was re-run (GPU 7, 10.3 s, 2026-10-20).

**G-PIPE is met.** At MX-2's geometry, Ec and residency, only depth varying:

| cell | depth 1 | depth 2 | depth 4 | depth 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 (Ec 256) | 5.958 | | 5.987 | 7.205 |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 7.773 | | 7.689 | |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 | 5.268 | 4.776 | **4.592** | |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 20.72 | | 16.789 | |

Depth helps where tasks hold long reductions (Llama-3.2-1B gate_up: 64 chunks
per task) and not where they hold 4-16 (Qwen3-0.6B B1); depth 6 on 16x32
spills (stack 232 → 376 B, +21%). The probe agrees: 3.34x at one CTA per AP,
1.5x at four. Twice I set the domain on too little evidence: depth 1 only
(from Qwen B1 alone; cost 8B 30%), then a 32-VGPR budget (held 8B's 16x64
tiles to depth 2, 5% behind MX-2). Two final runs were discarded for it.

## 4. Decode attention through MMA (MX3-3, G-ATTN)

`PrefillAttentionMma` at Tokens=1 already carried the decode contract, so
mma16 is a selection, not a new body; it is priced from its own fits and
refuses to compile without them, and its 11.5-38.5 KiB of shared memory (SIMT:
~2 KiB) caps residency at 2 for D=128. C-1/C-2 pass on all four development
cells (0 mismatches; C-1 max gap 0.016-0.34) once `check_modes.py` stopped
keeping each mode's 32 GB engine alive into the next. A/B, depth 1, MX-2's Ec:

| cell | simt | mma16 | residency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-0.6B B1 | 5.890 | 6.191 (+5.1%) | 2 |
| Qwen3-0.6B B16 | 8.354 | 13.376 (+60%) | 2 |
| Llama-3.2-1B B1 (D64 Q4) | 5.268 | **5.130 (−2.6%)** | 4 |
| Llama-3.1-8B B1 | 24.75 | 24.85 (+0.4%) | 2 |

mma16 wins only where D=64 lets it keep residency 4. **G-ATTN is not met**:
`TILEMEGA_ATTENTION_IMPL` is a whole-plan macro chosen on the command line, so
the search cannot weigh it per cell, and the final run keeps simt.

## 5. Execution and synchronisation (MX3-4)

**Residency (1)**: plans already run 3-4 per AP, so F8's 2-wave regime is not
there; 4 → 2 costs Qwen B1 3.9% and 8B 19%. **Barrier (2)**: the smallest
term (3-5%); unchanged, so no litmus. **LA (3)**: builds again; at matched
residency 3 it costs **+4.3-4.9%** from the protocol (+0.4% from residency),
and one of three runs hit a device `Trap Assert`. Off. **Epilogue (4)**: 4-5%
of the critical span; not attempted. **Partition (5)**: less split-K is slower
(Llama-3.2-1B 5.347 → 5.758 → 6.557 unsplit; Qwen B1 +4.8%).

## 6. Selection (MX3-5)

Decode is chosen by measurement: at most 4 candidates, one pilot round, the
top 2 timed twice (`--measure-finalists`, `--measure-rounds`); prefill keeps
the predicted choice. Pilots spread up to 1.7x, and on Qwen B1 the model's
first choice lost (5.57 vs 5.36 ms). A shortlisted plan with position-dependent
placement used to abort the whole search; it is now `MATERIALIZE_FAILED` and
dropped.

## 8. Reference ceilings (MX3-7)

**Vendor BLAS** (`F.linear`, as vLLM-MetaX calls it; `MX3_vendor_gemm_c550.csv`):
small decode GEMMs floor at ~13 µs, large ones reach 99-103% of 1.61 TB/s;
TileMega's TM-MX2 stage for the same class took 2.0-2.35x that on the small
models and 1.21-1.94x on 8B. **mcTlass** (`MX3_mctlass_c550.csv`): MX-2 timed
its defaults, a SIMT GEMM on Sm70. The tensor-op path does not assemble on
MACA (PTX `ldmatrix`, `arch/memory_sm75.h:250`), native mainloops are int8,
w4a16 and grouped/MoE bf16 only, and SIMT tuned for M ≤ 16 reaches 2.5% of
DRAM; `-O3` still crashes the host. Its native kernels wait with
`arrive_gvmcnt(n*(stages-1))` on register-staged B, as MX3-2 does.
