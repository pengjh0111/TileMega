# SM90R3 on H800: native dense + MoE LLM decode, and the DNN groundwork

Scope: prompt `TileMega_SM90R3_prompt.md`
(`882f1944130e9ea1b9a8dbb2741c57df52203d17af65aac26b0948f7e1eb22b3`), with
`SM90R2_review.md` (`2dc4b4763f35061bc78b12330c7e5bea083e3236cc302b934204a57e7511eba5`)
and `SM90R3_plan.md` (`70038bcac417b0aeec3ccc206460e09d33ba2222676f495c08254015a263c576`).
Baseline `d08ffeea` (tag `sm90r2-closeout-20261024`). This report covers the LLM
half (§14 items 1-8) through checkpoint CP-V; Phase N is in progress and its
items 9-10 follow in a later revision.

All ratios are against the frozen historical baselines
(`docs/results/R16_final_baselines.csv`), per engine, with no anchor and no
re-measurement of any baseline.

## 1. Synchronization note (§0.4 summary)

Four pipelines reach the GPU. Dense decode: `export.py` -> `ModelPlan.cpp`
(native: consumer-side RMS, shared layer scratch, per-layer K/V views) ->
the native solver (rule-pruned space, fixed per-stage formulas over measured
constants, measured top-4) -> tmexec coordinates -> `Codegen.cpp`'s typed
program -> one kernel launch per request, timed through the bench guard and
scheduler. Dense prefill keeps R2's closeout options. MoE decode shares that
path with six stages per layer; its prefill stays R1's legacy binary. DNN
forward still runs R1's legacy interpretive body. F1-F20 were checked against
`d08ffeea`; F8 and F10-F14 differ in detail from the prompt's text and were
recorded in `runs/sm90r3/sync/onboarding.md` without redesign, on the user's
instruction. Two measurement facts shaped the round: `ncu` is unavailable in
this container (`ERR_NVGPUCTRPERM`), so stage ledgers replace it everywhere a
profile was requested, and the cards are shared, so the guard refuses to start
or accept a timing run whenever a foreign process holds the card.

## 2. Decision tables

`SM90R3_decisions.csv` (T6) carries each table's input, measurement and branch.
In brief: **DT-0** never completed (its cohort cards were taken by another user
in all three attempts), so timing stayed serial until the user authorized
parallel cards; every such run is marked `NUMA_CONCURRENCY_TRIAL=1`.
**DT-1** found D6's cross-stage weight stream slower on 4 of 4 cells (+3.3 to
+18.7%), so it is off. **DT-2** measured C* = 2 at residency 1, which opened
D10b/D10c; only cluster 2 is offered because 4 and 8 move the grid off the
measured 132/264. **DT-3** found the layer loop never slower, so it is adopted.
**DT-4** saves 0.36 us at G264 but only 0.09 us at G132: read as passed where
the solver can choose that grid, with D8 priced per grid and P-SEL deciding
(judgement call). **DT-5** found LL polling slower at every size, so D10d was
not built. **DT-6** maps every DNN stage to an N-1 design item with a 0%
unexpected share, so no stop condition fired.

## 3. What was built

L0 rebuilt the solver on 2 KB of measured constants, replacing R2's 5.9 MB
development target (deleted with its provenance), added the one-wave attention
chunk rule (D1) and the parallel argmax (D4a). L1 replaced three bodies: GEMM
activations now arrive by TMA (D3), the epilogue accumulates RMS statistics in
the consumers and prefetches residual tiles (D4), and one swizzled mma.sync
attention body covers B 1-16, D 64/128, Q 2-8 (D2). L2 split shared memory
into two arenas with a ring rule (D5) and runs the layers as a typed loop with
shared scratch and stacked K/V maps (D7). L3 added the qkv->attention group
wait (D8), folded argmax and optional embedding into the lm_head last arriver
(D9), and the DSMEM attention merge and activation multicast under a cluster
launch (D10b/D10c). Phase M made MoE decode native: six stages per layer, 291
per step, with the router's last arriver producing the schedule, a grouped
gate_up that gathers rows by `cp.async`, and a down stage whose per-tile last
arriver combines the experts. H800 now refuses a legacy build for both dense
and MoE decode (G-REPLACE).

## 4. Final configuration per cell

`configs/serving/sm90r3/<model>_b<batch>.json` holds all 30 accepted cells:
the solve command, the solver's selected coordinates (geometry, attention
chunks/slots/merge, ring slots, pre-issue, residency, cluster, group wait,
embedding placement), the source HEAD, and the generated-source and binary
hashes. Stages per step: dense 5L + 2 (143 for Qwen0.6B, 163 for Llama1B,
83 for Llama3B/Llama8B at their layer counts), MoE 6L + 3 = 291, one fewer
when the embedding folds into the head. No coordinate was fixed by hand and
none was inherited across batches.

## 7. Solver quality

`SM90R3_solver.csv` (T4): 4 candidates per cell, 120 rows. tau = measured /
predicted has median 1.143 (range 1.059-1.468), so the fixed formulas
under-predict by 6-47% while ranking usefully. The measured winner was the
predicted top-1 in 8 of 30 cells and the selected candidate was the fastest
measured in 16 of 30; in the rest the top two were inside P-SEL's threshold
and the rule took the predicted-better one. No model was re-fitted and no
threshold gates the round, as §1.4 requires. `SM90R3_constants.csv` (T5)
carries the final constants with their method; `SM90R3_constants_L0.csv` and
`_L1.csv`, `_L3.csv` keep the earlier measurements for comparison. c_bar is
unchanged end to end (768/768/1280 ns at G 128/132/264); L2 latency moved
418 -> 421 ns and per-CTA stream bandwidth 65.8 -> 65.3 GB/s.

## 8. MoE

291 stages per step against REF-R2's 579-676, as designed. P-CORR passes on
both development cells and all five acceptance cells. The teacher-forced
routing check reaches 90.7% exact top-8 agreement with HF at B1 and 91.3% at
B16; every mismatch swaps one expert at the rank 8/9 boundary where HF's two
logits are 0-8 bf16 steps apart, and the frozen legacy decode scores 90.6% on
the same check. The prompt expects exact agreement and treats non-ties as
defects, so this is recorded as a difference rather than a pass: it is a
property of near-uniform random-checkpoint routers under bf16 rounding, shared
by both bodies, not a native defect. M8 attributes the frozen MoE prefill's
TTFT: at B1 182.2 ms with expert down 97.6 and gate_up 61.1 ms (87.5% of the
step against a 19 ms weight bound), at B16 1506.8 ms with 88.1% in the same
two stages.

## 9. Deviations and unmet items

- **DT-0 never ran**, so parallel timing rests on the user's authorization, not
  on the table; all such runs carry `NUMA_CONCURRENCY_TRIAL=1`. Cells measured
  before the switch keep their serial records.
- **GPU5 was promoted to a timing card** on the same authorization (the prompt
  reserves it for functional work); GPU6 was never used.
- **MoE prefills for B >= 2** use R1's gamma-folded DN binaries, not the
  closeout's, because the native decode and the prefill must share one copy of
  the expert pages and two differently packed copies exceed 80 GB. TPOT is
  unaffected; MoE TTFT is not comparable with the closeout's.
- **MoE expert pages keep the prefill's 64x64 tiling** rather than §8's
  per-TN packing, for the same memory reason; M5/M6 assemble their slots from
  page runs.
- **D5 left Qwen0.6B B16 2.9% slower than REF1** (U6: the smaller arena caps
  attention at 3 K/V slots). D5 is not a body replacement and has no decision
  table, so this was recorded, not stopped on.
- **DT-4's gate is grid-dependent** and was read as passed; D8 is a solver
  dimension rather than an unconditional change.
- **ncu is unavailable**, so no bank-conflict metric was measured for D2 and
  no ncu report accompanies any slower result.
- **D10a and D10d were not built**, having failed their gates; D10c is
  implemented and selected only where the solver prices it (Qwen0.6B B16).
- **Phase N** is under way: N-0 and DT-6 are complete and N-1's bodies and
  solver exist, but no DNN model is native yet, so §14 items 9-10 are absent.

## 10. Reproduction

Every accepted cell rebuilds from this HEAD: `configs/serving/sm90r3/` holds
the solve command and the coordinates, `scripts/sm90r3_solve.py` re-runs the
selection, and `scripts/sm90r3_llm.py` drives P-PAIR/P-SEL/P-FINAL/P-CORR
through the guard and scheduler. Raw measurements, traces, generated sources
and binaries stay under `runs/sm90r3/` and are not committed.
