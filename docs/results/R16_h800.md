## Reproduction and data

Raw evidence, failed attempts, generated CUDA, binaries, checkpoints and traces
remain under ignored `runs/r16/`. Random shared checkpoints use memfd shards,
released after the supervised process; no persistent BF16 checkpoint is needed.
Backend-native dummy checkpoints are explicitly marked as having unestablished
cross-system weight identity; MoE routing-derived MBU cannot be inferred.

Generate standalone baseline queues with `scripts/run_r16_baselines.py`.
Run each via `python -m tilemega.bench.scheduler`, with explicit physical
`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=i`, `TILEMEGA_DEVICE_INDEX=i` and card lock environment.
Strict ownership is the reproduction default. `scripts/summarize_r16.py`
collects only accepted completed 64/1024 runs and reports fresh-process counts.
The shared-checkpoint TileMega entry is `python -m tilemega.serving.r16_run`.

Completed three-process cells, including diagnostic TileMega arms, are in
`R16_llm_complete.csv`;
all are ownership-provisional. Incomplete cells remain raw. Full-matrix
ratios, geometric means, MBU, defaults and final gate decisions are pending. Multi-GPU execution is out of scope;
