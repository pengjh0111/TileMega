## 0. Synchronization note, in ten lines (§0.4)

## 3. Phase B: tensor parallel implementation

Single process, T devices, one plan per rank from one generated library, CUDA
P2P (plan §3.2). o and down reduce their tile inside the epilogue as 8-byte LL
packets carrying (two bf16 values, epoch); the head exchanges one (value,
global index) per row, and its release/acquire also orders the next step's
writes after this step's reads, so the AR path carries no fence (plan §3.5 C2).

TP1 is unchanged by construction: the degree, AR slot and head vocabulary are
written only when `tp > 1`, so a TP1 coordinate still serializes as schema 6
with byte-identical generated source.

### 3.1 Correctness (verified)

| check | TP2 | TP4 | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR + head exchange unit test, bitwise | pass | pass | `sm90_dense_tp_test`, 8 AR cases (both epilogue paths, TN 8/16/32, B1/B16) + 3 head cases |
| mutation control | fails as designed | — | reversing the tie rule and dropping per-rank bf16 rounding gives 4096 errors |
| litmus, 1024 tokens, 5 launches | 5/5 identical | 5/5 identical | perturbed step 1.515 vs 1.227 ms (TP2), 1.353 vs 1.075 ms (TP4) |
| C-1 vs HF, 1024 positions | pass | pass | B1 and B16 per degree, every position inside the near-tie threshold |
| C-2, 1024 tokens, 5 launches | bitwise identical | bitwise identical | B1 and B16 per degree; executor loop and per-launch give the same tokens |
| per-rank token agreement | enforced | enforced | asserted by `TpServingEngine` on every generation; dense gate byte-identical at every commit |

C-1 maximum gaps: 0.03125 at TP2 B1/B16 and TP4 B16, 0.015625 at TP4 B1 — one
bf16 step or less against a single-card HF reference at all 1024 positions
(`SM90R5_tp_final.csv` has the per-cell verdicts).

The executor comparison found a real defect in the harness, not the device
code: `TpServingEngine` enqueued one rank's whole step range first, but a
step-s kernel spins in its first all-reduce until every peer's step-s kernel
runs, so the launch queue filled and the host blocked with the peers never
enqueued. Fixed by enqueueing one step across all ranks before advancing (128
tokens in 9.2 s, tokens identical to the loop path). No measured cell is
affected: every TPOT uses the step loop, one kernel per rank.

Every P-FINAL median agrees with its P-SEL median to within 0.04%, every spread
is inside the 0.5% floor, and TileMega is 1.20–1.61× the fastest engine at each
degree (TRT-LLM everywhere). Scaling efficiency TP1/(T × TPOT) is 0.55–0.66 at
TP2, 0.32–0.37 at TP4: 1.12× and 1.28× over TP1 at B1.

The prompt's inferred targets (TP2 B1 1.00–1.15 ms, TP4 B1 0.75–0.95 ms at
1.4–2.0×) are not met on absolute time at any cell; §4 is the measured reason.

**MPK ships no working sm90 TP build.** `tasks/hopper/task_header.cuh` omits the
`tasks/hopper/allreduce.cuh` include defining `nvshmem_tile_allreduce`, which
its own Blackwell header carries guarded for exactly the non-Blackwell case, so
every TP graph fails to compile (`namespace "kernel" has no member
"nvshmem_tile_allreduce"`, verbatim under
`runs/sm90r5/mpk_tp/stock-mpk-tp2-build-failure/`), MPK's own demo_hopper.py TP
path included. The rows add that one include (§6); it changes no kernel. That
the authors left it unwired is consistent with what the path then does: **3 of
32 launches dropped a request** — always request 0, at step 922-947 of 1087, on
a repeat after an identical successful run; once as a short token table whose
peers then spun in the all-reduce. MPK is 1.70-2.06× TileMega and barely beats
its own TP1 (scaling efficiency 0.51 at TP2, 0.30 at TP4).

**torch.compile loses 5-9× at TP**, and TP4 (35.6-36.9) equals TP2 (36.6-39.9):
doubling the ranks changes nothing, so neither collectives nor GPU work is the
limit, and TPOT moves +0.7% from B1 to B16 at TP2 against +65% at TP1 — a fixed
host cost per token. `tp_plan`'s module-level backward hooks rule out fullgraph;
four frames then exhaust dynamo's `recompile_limit` (8) on a guard over
`q_proj.forward.__closure__[2].cell_contents`, the per-module forward closure TP
installs, after which they run eagerly. `reduce-overhead` runs at TP unchanged.
Tokens match the single card for 112 greedy steps (B1) before a near-tie
diverges, so the sharding is correct.

## 4. Open finding: the TP cost model under-predicts, and mis-ranks the tiles

All four measured candidates at TP2 B1 carry qkv TN16 and gate_up TN32 and
differ only in lm_head. A coordinate carried over from REF-R3's TP1 winner (qkv
TN32/3 slots/WK1, gate_up TN48) is 21.9% faster and never entered the top 4.
Both are legal (qkv TN32 gives 64 tasks at TP2, inside the admitted 33…264), so
this is a ranking error in `Sm90DenseCostModel`, not a pruning gap. Per §1.5 the
coordinate was not hand-fixed and the model not re-fitted; see the τ control.

Same protocol, group and checkpoint, 3 launches each, so only the coordinate
differs (verified): at TP2 B1 the solver winner is 1.4993 against **1.2302** for
the REF-R3-derived coordinate, a 21.9% gap at 0.159% spread (target 1.00–1.15);
at TP4 B1, 1.3079 against **1.0771**, 21.4% at 0.036% (target 0.75–0.95).

With the unranked coordinate TP4 B1 reaches 1.467× the fastest TP4 engine and
TP2 B1 1.584× — inside the round's 1.4–2.0× expectation, against 1.208× and
1.300× for the selected winners (TRT-LLM is fastest at every TP cell). The TP
mechanism therefore meets the ratio goals; the shortfall against §3.3's targets
is the cost model's ranking, 21–22% at both degrees. The same inversion appears
inside the solved cells: TP2 B4 (1.3887) beats TP2 B1 (1.4993) and TP4 B8
(1.2752) beats TP4 B1 (1.3079), although TPOT should rise with batch — at B4
and B8 the search reaches the wider gate_up and o tiles it misses at B1.

The model also under-predicts the step everywhere, and more so with more ranks
(`SM90R5_tp_solver.csv`, µs per step, B1→B16). TP2 predicts 1019.9 / 1036.0 /
1066.4 / 1113.6 / 1242.4 against 1499.3 / 1509.1 / 1388.7 / 1580.7 / 1584.4
measured, τ 1.470 / 1.457 / 1.302 / 1.419 / 1.275; TP4 predicts 791.9 / 824.0 /
842.0 / 906.8 / 1062.0 against 1307.9 / 1321.2 / 1349.4 / 1275.2 / 1528.3,
τ 1.652 / 1.603 / 1.603 / 1.406 / 1.439.

Mean τ 1.463. The cross-rank terms are not the cause: at TP4 B1 the measured
`t_ar` gives 56 × 1.728 µs = 97 µs and the exchange 3.5 µs against a 516 µs
shortfall, and the model's 791.9 µs reproduces the plan's own §3.10 estimate
(~740 µs) — the unaccounted time sits in the per-stage fixed cost at these
shapes, not in the TP mechanism. Localizing it needs D0 marks on a TP program.

## 6. Environment findings (verified)

- **Idle power recalibrated.** GPUs 0–4 hold 1980 MHz SM clocks while idle (set
  outside this harness), drawing 129.5–142.9 W against the 81.5–87.8 W recorded,
  so no group could start; idle values updated, no clock changed.
- **GPU 6 excluded from timing**, per user instruction, then re-scoped for TP8
  (§7).
- **Disclosed environment changes for MPK TP**: two dev symlinks in
  `/root/.venvs/nvshmem/nvidia/nvshmem/lib` (`libnvshmem_host.so` → `.so.3`,
  `nvshmem_bootstrap_mpi.so` → `.so.3`), and one include added to MPK,
  `scripts/mpk_patches/mpk-63af900-sm90-tp.patch`, copied from its own
  Blackwell header (§3.2).
- **MPK Qwen3-30B-A3B B16** is an MPK defect, not memory:
  `multitoken_paged_attention_hopper_impl` is wrong whenever MAX_TOKENS ×
  queries-per-KV exceeds 64 (16 × 8 here), reaching the argmax as NaN, whose
  `-1` equals the `eos_token_id=-1` sentinel, so every request stops at step 64.
  Localized on two-layer random models teacher-forced against HF: 16 slots match
  1–5 of 128 tokens, 8 slots 124 of 128 (gap ≤ 0.047, bf16 ties); a dense Qwen3
  with the same head counts fails identically and Llama-3.1-8B (4 queries per
  KV) is correct at 16 slots. B16 is therefore collected at the demo default of
  8 slots, 25.5895 ms, two kernel iterations per step — not like-for-like, and
  labelled so in `SM90R5_mpk.csv`.
- **OLMoE torch.compile** was never a torch.compile measurement in 4.57.1: no
  experts interface there, the per-expert `torch.where` breaks fullgraph on
  `aten.nonzero`, every expert iteration's resume frame recompiles to dynamo's
  limit (8) and the rest runs eagerly — 92–143 ms, host-CPU bound, moving 46%
  with host load. Under the R16 MoE setup (transformers 5.20.1, `grouped_mm`,
  fullgraph): 3.0361 / 3.9906 / 5.2785 / 7.5639 / 12.3558 ms, 1.83–2.42×
  TileMega (`SM90R5_olmoe_torchcompile.csv`).

## 7. TP8: functionally verified, timing withheld (verified)

Requested after TP2/TP4, with the question whether GPU 6 throttles constantly or
only once hot. Measured (`runs/sm90r5/tp/gpu6_thermal.json`, 10 segments of a
fixed bf16 matmul load with 15 s idle gaps):

- **Idle**: 80 °C, 1830 MHz, 194 W, every throttle reason inactive, against
  31–46 °C and 82–143 W on the other cards — it does not throttle constantly.
- **Under load**: `sw_thermal_slowdown` Active in **all 10 segments**, each only
  0.33–0.48 s long, at 89–90 °C, SM clocks 990–1635 MHz against the 1980 MHz
  its peers hold; one segment also raised `hw_thermal_slowdown` and
  `hw_slowdown`. Throughput spanned 342–494 TFLOPS, a 30.8% range.
- **Recovery**: 15 s idle restores 81–82 °C and 1830 MHz, then it re-throttles
  at once. Segmenting cannot avoid an onset faster than any useful unit of work
  (a 1024-token decode runs ~1.5 s), and §1.6 voids a throttled round anyway.
  No TP8 TPOT is published and the T = 8 constants were not measured.

Correctness does not depend on clocks, so the 8-rank protocol was verified at
fixed coordinates (per-rank qkv N 512 → TN8, gate_up N 1536, down K 768,
vocabulary 18992 → TN16; queries per KV head is 16/8 = 2 at every degree, so
the attention shape and its 64-chunk merge carry over):

The AR and head-exchange unit test gave 0 errors over 8 AR and 3 head cases at
8 ranks; C-1 against HF over 64 positions passed with max gap **0.0** at every
position; tokens were stable across 2 runs and identical on all 8 ranks every
generation; and the 1024-token litmus was **5/5 bitwise identical** (step
1.0755 → 1.3536 ms perturbed).

The eight ranks' kernels start within ~1.5 ms of each other and run
concurrently, so the single-process, T-device launch model holds at degree 8.
